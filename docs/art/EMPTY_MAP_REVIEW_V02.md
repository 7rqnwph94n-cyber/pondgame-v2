# Empty Verdant map — acceptance review v0.2

**Capture:** [`../milestone_b/captures/verdant_empty_map_v08.png`](../milestone_b/captures/verdant_empty_map_v08.png)
**Verdict:** **FAIL — 5.5/10. Buildings remain excluded.**

## Improvement over v0.1

- Fog wash removed from the diagnostic view.
- Water now has animated material variation and a stronger depth colour.
- The channel has a deliberately authored S-curve rather than reading as a straight strip.
- Wet-bank plants form five ecological clusters instead of an even scatter line.
- Silica, methane and sulphur each have a distinct landmark formation.
- The silica ridge has a sharper elevation face and associated geological scatter.
- Terrain extends safely beyond the camera frustum with no visible corners.

## Still below acceptance

- Procedural colour mottling is not a substitute for a proper sediment/mineral texture atlas.
- Cliff and resource meshes remain blockout quality.
- Vegetation families need larger massing, ground cover and boundary succession.
- The floodplain lacks deposits, shallow channels and seasonal evidence.
- Water lacks bank intersection foam/murk, suspended matter and visible flow direction.
- The scene needs stronger value separation between fertile lowland, stable terrace and mineral upland.
